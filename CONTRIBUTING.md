# Contributing to Ovumcy

Thanks for your interest in improving Ovumcy.

- **Bugs & features:** open a GitHub issue on the relevant repository.
- **Pull requests:** keep them scoped and atomic, describe the change and its motivation,
  add or adjust tests for behavioral changes, and make sure existing checks pass.
- **Project-specific rules:** some repositories define their own `CONTRIBUTING.md` with
  build/test instructions and contracts (for example, the `/api/v1/*` stability contract in
  ovumcy-web, and the zero-knowledge server contract in ovumcy-sync-community). Follow that
  when present.
- **Security issues:** never report these publicly — see `SECURITY.md`.
- **Code of Conduct:** by participating you agree to abide by our `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.

By contributing you agree that your contributions are licensed under AGPL v3.
